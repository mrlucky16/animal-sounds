Optional: real animal recordings
================================

The app makes every animal sound itself, so this folder can stay empty.

To use a real recording instead, add an MP3 named after the animal id:

  dog.mp3  cat.mp3  cow.mp3  duck.mp3  pig.mp3
  sheep.mp3  rooster.mp3  frog.mp3  lion.mp3  owl.mp3

Missing files simply fall back to the built-in sound. Good free sources:
freesound.org (check each licence) and Wikimedia Commons.
Short clips (1-3 seconds) work best.
